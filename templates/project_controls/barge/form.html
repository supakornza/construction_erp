How to link Equipment

The Linked Equipment field lets you connect this Transport Unit to an existing Equipment record.

  • Useful for barges, split barges, and trucks already registered in the Equipment module
  • Linking avoids double-entry — the Transport Unit appears in Rock/Sand forms while the same unit is tracked in Equipment daily records
  • Leave blank if this Transport Unit is not a registered equipment item
